	3  Corporate Sustainability Reports (CSR)
CSR PDFs are typically © the issuer; no blanket statutory exemption. However, large-scale text mining for research is recognised as transformative fair use (see Authors Guild v. Google, 804 F.3d 202 (2d Cir. 2015)).

	4  Data-Handling Controls
* Storage: S3 buckets raw/, processed/, features/, encryption-at-rest.
* Access: IAM roles (esg-etl, esg-ml, esg-dashboard) – least privilege.
* Retention: Raw filings kept up to 3 years (aligns with internal policy FIN-POL-17) or longer if required by regulation; derived features retained indefinitely.
* Redaction: If PII detected, scrub via spaCy NER before storage.
* Privacy Audit: Quarterly PII scan of raw corpus; results logged in Risk register.
